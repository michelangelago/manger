Food tracking through Telegram

Manage your food catalog, log consumption, and understand your calorie totals.

A Telegram bot for tracking food consumption, calories, and daily calorie goals. Manage a shared food catalog, log portions in grams, and view compact summaries for a day, week, month, or year.

Built with PHP, Nutgram, and SQLite. The bot uses long polling, so it does not need a public URL or web server.

A typical workflow

A typical workflow:

text
/start
/food -C "Green apple" 52 fruits
/eat "Green apple" 150
/goal -C 2000
/dview
  • Food calories are entered per 100 grams. Consumed portions are positive whole grams; calories are calculated as energy × grams / 100.
  • Food and group names ignore letter case. Quote names containing spaces.
  • Foods and groups form a shared catalog. Daily logs, consumption entries, and calorie goals belong to individual users.
  • The first consumed-food entry creates that user's daily log for today. Further entries reuse it. Days without consumption do not create logs.
  • Editing a consumption entry keeps its original date and time. Deleting all entries keeps the daily log available for reuse that day.
  • Creating catalog foods with /food -C does not create daily logs.
